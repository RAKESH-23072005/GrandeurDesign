Our Story

Architects and engineers, designing from R.S. Puram, Coimbatore

The Grandeur Design is a firm of architects and engineers providing complete building-related solutions and design services. We believe architecture is a visual art — a building should communicate its purpose — and we carry that belief into every residence, office, café, hospital and commercial space we design.

From working plans and approval drawings to elevations, electrical and plumbing layouts, furniture layouts and flooring details, our experienced team supports clients from the first site visit to the finished space.
