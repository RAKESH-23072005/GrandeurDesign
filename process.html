How We Work

A clear five-step journey

From understanding your land to handing over room-wise interior drawings — with your feedback shaping every stage.

1

Understand & Visit

We collect your requirements and vision for the land or space, then physically visit the site to understand conditions and practical possibilities.

2

Design Layouts

Our architects and designers prepare detailed design layouts, separately considering each floor or storey of the building.

3

Electrical & Plumbing

Detailed electrical and plumbing layouts are prepared for each floor so the building functions properly from day one.

4

Elevation Drawings

Building elevation drawings are created with all four sides represented, communicating the intended exterior appearance.

5

Interiors & Revisions

Room-specific interior drawings are prepared, presented to you for feedback, and revised based on your suggestions.

Full Documentation Scope

Everything your builder needs

Alongside the five core steps, we prepare working plans, approval plans, building elevations and façades, electrical, plumbing and lighting layouts, furniture layouts and flooring details.

What to prepare for your first consultation

  • Site address and any existing drawings
  • Your requirements room by room
  • Budget range and preferred timeline
  • Design references you love (photos, links)
